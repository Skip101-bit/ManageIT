BOM
Upload a BOM for one job, check it against the master inventory, then run the stock check.
The master inventory is not changed by a BOM upload. The job number below is applied to every part and quantity in the file. Parts that are not on the master are flagged and are not allocated until you add them to the master. Adding them creates the part at quantity 0.

C&I part numbers are updated in the Parts Library. Other AutoCAD parts are updated in AutoCAD. Other Parts Library parts are updated in the Parts Library. Import columns: part number, description, qty.

Part #DescriptionBOM qtyFree stockStatus

The stock check uses free stock only. Stock already allocated to a job is not counted. Allocating free stock to the job does not create an order transaction and does not change the total quantity.
